# Possible Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-04

This policy explains how **BRAIN TRUST PRIVATE LIMITED**, a Singapore company (UEN 202416698H) ("**we**", "**us**"), collects, uses and
discloses personal data through possible.sh, the Possible command-line tool, and the agent prompts and Outcomes you can use or buy there (the
"**Service**"). We're responsible for that data under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and, where they apply, the EU and UK
GDPR.

Prompts run on your own agent and computer. We don't receive what you give your agent or what it produces.

## What we collect

- **Order details.** Link (Sold through Link, LLC, a Stripe company) sells you your purchase as merchant of record. It collects your payment
  details, billing address and any tax information at checkout, and uses them as a separate controller under its own
  [privacy policy](https://link.com/privacy). Link shares order details with us, such as your name, email address, billing country, what you
  bought and payment status, but not your full card number. We also keep a record of your download.
- **Your account, if you sign in with GitHub.** Signing in is optional. It lets you save and like Outcomes across devices. When you allow it,
  GitHub shares your GitHub name, email address, profile picture link and GitHub user ID with us, and we keep them with a record of your
  sign-in sessions. Your browser keeps your sign-in token in local storage.
- **Saves and likes.** Which Outcomes you save or like, and when.
- **Usage counts.** When you copy or run a free Outcome, on the website or with the command-line tool, we record which Outcome, the date and time,
  and whether it came from the website or the tool, with a scrambled (hashed) form of a random ID. That ID is created once and kept in your
  browser's local storage or in a file on your computer. It isn't linked to your account, your name or your email address.
- **What you publish.** You can publish an Outcome without an account. Its source, such as a GitHub repository or web address, and a
  publisher name are shown publicly. The publisher name is the one you give or, if you don't give one, the GitHub account or website name.
- **Communications**, such as messages you send us.
- **Technical information** that our hosting provider processes to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address and browser type.

Our page-visit counts are anonymous daily totals by page, referring site and campaign tag. They don't use cookies and don't identify you.

## How we use it

We use personal data to:

- deliver your download, help you with it and handle refunds;
- sign you in and keep your saves and likes;
- count how many people use each Outcome each day, and limit abuse;
- show who published an Outcome;
- keep order and accounting records;
- reply to you;
- protect the Service and prevent fraud and abuse;
- improve the Service;
- comply with the law and enforce our terms.

**Legal bases (EU and UK).** We rely on: performance of our contract with you; our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving the
Service; your consent, where we ask for it; and compliance with legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal data.

## Who we share it with

- **Link**, to handle your order, payment questions and refunds.
- **GitHub**, when you choose to sign in with it. GitHub handles your GitHub account under its own
  [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Service providers** that help us run the Service: hosting and content delivery, database and email delivery. They may use the data only to
  provide their services to us.
- **Authorities and others**, where the law requires it, or where we believe it's needed to protect the rights, property or safety of Possible,
  our users or others.
- **A buyer or successor**, if we sell or transfer all or part of our business.

We may share aggregated or de-identified information that doesn't identify you.

## International transfers

We're based in Singapore, and our service providers may process data in other countries. Where we transfer personal data out of Singapore, we
take steps to ensure it receives protection comparable to the PDPA. For data from the EU or UK, we use a lawful transfer mechanism, such as
standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- **Account data** stays until you ask us to delete your account.
- **Saves and likes** are deleted when you remove them, or when we delete your account.
- **Usage counts** are kept only as long as we need them to count use and limit abuse.
- **Published Outcomes**, including their source details and the versions we've stored, are kept while they're listed and for as long as
  we need them afterwards to run the Service. To have one removed, email **hello@possible.sh**.
- **Order records** are kept as long as we need them to handle refunds, resolve disputes and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

After that, we delete or anonymise data within a reasonable time. Copies may remain for a limited period in backups.

To delete your account, email **hello@possible.sh** from the email address on your GitHub account. To remove the random ID, clear your
browser's site data for possible.sh, or delete the tool's file on your computer
(`~/.possible/installation.json`). To stop the tool sending usage counts, set `POSSIBLE_TELEMETRY=0`.

## Security

We use reasonable security arrangements to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't
guarantee the security of your data.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object
to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email **hello@possible.sh**. We may need to verify your identity, and we'll respond
within the time the law requires. Some data may be exempt, for example where we must keep it by law.

You can also complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority.

## Children

The Service isn't for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete
it.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here and update the date above. Where the law requires, we'll tell you
about material changes in another way too.

## Contact

Questions and requests: **hello@possible.sh**. You can contact our Data Protection Officer at the same address.
