## When we refund you

- **It doesn't work as described.** If a prompt doesn't do what its page says when run with a supported agent, tell us within 60 days of
  buying it and say what happened. We'll help, or fix it, and if we can't, we refund you in full.
- **You can't download it.** If the download doesn't work and we can't fix it, we refund you in full.
- **Wrong charge.** If you're charged twice for the same prompt or bundle, or charged without your permission, we refund that payment.

## Otherwise

Because a prompt is a download you keep, we don't refund because you changed your mind, didn't like the output, or because of the cost of
running the prompt on your own agent or model, unless Link's terms or the law give you that right.

## How to ask

Email **hello@possible.sh** from the address you used at checkout, or contact Link support from your receipt. Either we or Link can issue the
refund. Refunds go back to your original payment method, including any tax you paid. How long it takes to reach you depends on your bank or
card issuer. Once an order is refunded, your licence to the prompts in it ends, and you must delete them.

## Link's terms and your legal rights

Link's [Sold through Link terms](https://link.com/terms#sold-through-link-terms) and refund policy also apply to your purchase. If they give you
more than this policy, for example a cooling-off right for consumers in the EU or UK, you have that too. This policy doesn't affect any rights
the law where you live gives you that can't be excluded, including rights when digital content is faulty.
