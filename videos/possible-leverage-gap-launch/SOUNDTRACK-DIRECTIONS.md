# Soundtrack directions

## Current direction — Garden Window

`Garden Window` is a complete 24-cycle Strudel composition with its own musical
arc: a gentle piano motif opens, harmony and bass gradually widen the piece,
then it returns to a quieter closing thought. The film uses cycles 4–24 as a
55-second excerpt, preserving the complete final phrase. It was evaluated and exported by the official Codeberg
Strudel application, using Strudel's piano, supersaw, voicing, modulation,
delay, and room effects.

- Source: `score/garden-window.strudel.js`
- Official Strudel export: `assets/audio/garden-window-official.wav`
- Review master: `assets/audio/garden-window.wav`
- Compact preview: `assets/audio/garden-window.m4a`
- Verified: 55 seconds, stereo 44.1 kHz, -18.6 LUFS, 16.3 LU range,
  -3.2 dBFS true peak

The master differs from the official export only by a transparent listening-
level lift and short edge fades. It is now selected in the editable film source;
the previous silent render remains preserved alongside the final soundtrack
version. `Quiet World` and the three earlier directions remain only as
comparison history.

Three original Strudel scores audition the same 44-second film. They share the
same story boundaries and are mastered to the same review loudness, so the
choice is about identity rather than volume.

## 01 — Editorial pulse

Precise FM marks, warm low pulses, and restrained open harmony. It treats the
motion like kinetic typography: minimal at first, organized when Possible
appears, and clean on the call to action.

## 02 — Curious discovery

A light question-and-answer motif with asymmetric movement and buoyant bass.
It feels exploratory and modular without becoming cute, game-like, or busy.

## 03 — Restrained cinematic lift

Open major harmony, patient pulses, and one earned melodic rise as the Outcome
Pack unfolds. It feels larger and more emotional, while avoiding ominous drones
and generic trailer weight.

## Review rule

Choose the direction that best changes how the film feels. Refinement happens
only after that choice; these are not intended to be blended together.
