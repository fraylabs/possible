# Possible — The world inside Codex

workflow: product-launch-video
flow: autonomous

## Intent

- **Audience:** Codex users and AI-curious makers who currently think of Codex mainly as a coding agent.
- **Launch message:** Codex can make far more than most people know to ask for. Possible reveals that larger world of capabilities and turns each possibility into an Outcome Pack that is ready to run.
- **Finished result:** A short, diagrammatic launch film that expands the viewer's mental model of Codex, names the discovery problem plainly, introduces Possible as both the map and the practical route into one chosen outcome, and closes by revealing that its original soundtrack is itself another Codex + Possible outcome.
- **Duration:** 55 seconds at 1920×1080.
- **Destination:** Product launch, website hero, and social sharing.
- **Source boundary:** Self-contained HTML and CSS. No screenshots, fake Codex UI, recorded session, narration, or external runtime.
- **Content status:** The 1×/100× contrast is a visual framing for capability awareness, not a measured productivity claim. Possible is presented as a growing library, never as a complete inventory of everything Codex can do.
- **Audio:** `Garden Window`, an original 55-second Strudel soundtrack composed with Codex + Possible, exported through the official local Strudel application, and selected after human listening review. No voiceover or decorative sound effects.
- **External actions:** None. Do not publish, upload, deploy, or contact an audience.

## Creative direction

- **Primary visual idea:** Begin inside one small code window, then pull back until it is only one object in a much larger capability world. That world becomes an organized Possible library. One independent Outcome Pack unfolds to reveal its complete prompt, selected skills, and expectations checklist.
- **Tone:** Clean, tasteful, editorial, confident, and legible to someone who has never heard of Outcome Packs.
- **Palette:** Warm paper, ink, orange signal, muted green for completed work, and a restrained blue accent for the capability field.
- **Motion:** Camera pullback, center-outward expansion, independent artifact tiles locking into a library, and one card unfolding. Motion explains the story; it does not decorate slides.
- **Story rhythm:** familiar view → capability explosion → awareness gap → Possible reveal → how one possibility runs → invitation → the soundtrack proves one more possibility.

## Acceptance surface

- A new viewer can answer three questions after one viewing: what is being missed, what Possible does, and what they should do next.
- The capability examples read as independent possibilities, not stages in a workflow.
- Product mechanics appear only after the promise and are explained in plain language before internal terms.
- `index.html` remains the editable source of truth and replays deterministically.
- The rendered film contains the selected `Garden Window` master for the full 55 seconds and clearly identifies the soundtrack as another Codex + Possible outcome.
- Representative snapshots and a high-quality MP4 accompany the source.
