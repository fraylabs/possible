## Video direction

- Rhythm: deliberate hook → expanding reveal → held insight → organized reveal → satisfying unfold → quiet CTA → self-proving soundtrack reveal.
- Keep the capability tiles visually distinct. They are independent outcomes, not steps or a pipeline.
- The line “You can only ask for possibilities you know exist” is the plain-language problem statement.
- Use “complete instructions / selected skills / checklist for done” as the public explanation. “Long prompt / skills / expectations” may appear as small secondary labels only.
- The capability world is growing, not exhaustive. Use “and more” rather than claiming Possible maps every possible outcome.
- Use the approved `Garden Window` master across all seven beats. Add no voiceover, clicks, drones, or decorative sound effects.
