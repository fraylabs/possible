# Possible — HTML/CSS Motion Film

workflow: motion-graphics
flow: autonomous

## Intent

- **Audience:** People using Codex who have an open-ended idea and need a clear next outcome.
- **Finished result:** A short, silent motion film that explains Possible as the narrowing and verification layer between a rough request and one inspectable outcome.
- **Primary visual idea:** A branching request resolves into a three-part Outcome Pack, then into an Expectations checklist.
- **Duration:** 31.8 seconds at 1920×1080.
- **Source boundary:** The authored source is self-contained HTML and CSS. It does not reproduce a live website, invent a working UI, or require a JavaScript runtime.
- **Content status:** Product language is grounded in the local Possible README, pack contract, and docs. The branching request and checklist are illustrative explanations of the contract, not claims about a completed customer run.
- **Audio:** Silent by design. Sound is outside this HTML/CSS-only pack's promise.
- **External actions:** None. Do not publish, upload, deploy, or contact an audience.

## Motion language

Editorial and deliberate: a request drifts through a branching field, one outcome locks in, three primitives assemble, and evidence resolves the story. Use restrained paper texture, a single orange accent, and varied entrances (slide, rise, draw, assemble) rather than a slideshow of screenshots.

## Acceptance surface

- `index.html` remains the editable source of truth.
- The film starts from a known state and replays deterministically.
- Desktop and narrow browser review preserve readable hierarchy; reduced motion preserves the message as a static composition.
- Representative start, middle, end, and replay captures plus a completion checklist accompany the source.
