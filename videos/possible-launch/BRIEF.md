## Intent

Market Possible to builders who can already ask an agent to make things but need a
clearer way to coordinate the work, inspect the result, and separate artifacts from
proof. The film should feel like a restrained product launch: one idea, real screens,
clean typography, and no presentation-deck pacing.

## Assets

- http://localhost:3000 — the current local Possible site; capture the homepage, docs, and an Outcome Pack page as the visual source of truth.
- The local Possible repository — source for confirmed product language and the three authoring primitives: long prompt, reviewed Skills, and Expectations.

## Customizations

- Use the official HyperFrames product-launch-video workflow and its pinned HyperFrames skill.
- Lead with the viewer's problem: an agent can produce an artifact without making the outcome inspectable.
- Show the real site and docs, especially the Expectations checklist and run-evidence boundary.
- Keep the film text-led and silent (`music: none`); do not add filler ambience, generated music, or unexplained sound effects.

## Notes

- Treat the local site and repository as the source of truth; do not invent customer results, metrics, testimonials, or product capabilities.
