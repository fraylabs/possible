[strong] CORE CONCEPTS / EXPECTATIONS & EVIDENCE
[p] CORE CONCEPTS
[h1] The artifact is not the proof.
[p] Expectations make the definition of done explicit. Evidence records what was observed. Verification decides whether the active expectations are actually true.
[span] Copy page
[h2] The four layers
[p] Keeping these terms separate prevents a polished deliverable from being mistaken for a completed outcome.
[span] OUTPUT
[strong] The inspectable artifact
[span] EXPECTATION
[strong] What must become true
[span] EVIDENCE
[strong] What was observed or preserved
[span] VERIFICATION
[strong] Why the claim can be trusted
[span] OUTPUT
[p] A responsive launch page with a working conversion flow.
[span] EXPECTATION
[p] The confirmed audience can complete the flow on supported screen sizes, approved claims are used, and accessibility checks pass.
[h2] Required and preferred expectations
[p] A pack can include required and preferred checklist items. Each item is explicit and observable; no hidden module or domain-specific schema creates work outside the prompt, Skills, and Expectations.
[span] REQUIRED
[strong] Completion blockers
[p] If an active required expectation fails, the run cannot honestly close. Preserve the failure, repair the result, and rerun the complete review.
[span] PREFERRED
[strong] Quality signals
[p] Useful improvements that inform the completion report without pretending they are blockers when the approved contract did not require them.
[h2] What a complete review does
[strong] Inspect the integrated artifact
[span] Review the result as one coherent outcome, not as isolated specialist handoffs.
[strong] Challenge every active expectation
[span] Use the appropriate tests, measurements, review, or fixture and preserve the evidence used.
[strong] Record failures and limitations
[span] Do not silently downgrade a failed check or turn an unproven claim into a success.
[strong] Repair and rerun
[span] After a material repair, repeat the complete review so the new result is freshly verified.
[h2] Outcome Record
[p] Every run exposes one machine-readable Outcome Record. It connects the approved brief and pack snapshot to artifacts, hashes, expectation results, decisions, failures, repairs, approvals, limitations, and fresh verification.
[strong] COMPLETION REPORT
[p] The final report says what was produced, what was checked, what remains unproven, and which external actions were intentionally not taken.
