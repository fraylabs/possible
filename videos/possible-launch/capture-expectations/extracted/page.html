DOCS/CORE CONCEPTS / EXPECTATIONS & EVIDENCE

CORE CONCEPTS

The artifact is not the proof.

Expectations make the definition of done explicit. Evidence records what was observed. Verification decides whether the active expectations are actually true.

The four layers

Keeping these terms separate prevents a polished deliverable from being mistaken for a completed outcome.

  1. OUTPUTThe inspectable artifact
  2. EXPECTATIONWhat must become true
  3. EVIDENCEWhat was observed or preserved
  4. VERIFICATIONWhy the claim can be trusted
OUTPUT

A responsive launch page with a working conversion flow.

EXPECTATION

The confirmed audience can complete the flow on supported screen sizes, approved claims are used, and accessibility checks pass.

Required and preferred expectations

A pack can include required and preferred checklist items. Each item is explicit and observable; no hidden module or domain-specific schema creates work outside the prompt, Skills, and Expectations.

REQUIREDCompletion blockers

If an active required expectation fails, the run cannot honestly close. Preserve the failure, repair the result, and rerun the complete review.

PREFERREDQuality signals

Useful improvements that inform the completion report without pretending they are blockers when the approved contract did not require them.

What a complete review does

  1. Inspect the integrated artifactReview the result as one coherent outcome, not as isolated specialist handoffs.
  2. Challenge every active expectationUse the appropriate tests, measurements, review, or fixture and preserve the evidence used.
  3. Record failures and limitationsDo not silently downgrade a failed check or turn an unproven claim into a success.
  4. Repair and rerunAfter a material repair, repeat the complete review so the new result is freshly verified.

Outcome Record

Every run exposes one machine-readable Outcome Record. It connects the approved brief and pack snapshot to artifacts, hashes, expectation results, decisions, failures, repairs, approvals, limitations, and fresh verification.

COMPLETION REPORT

The final report says what was produced, what was checked, what remains unproven, and which external actions were intentionally not taken.
