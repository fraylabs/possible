## Video direction

Use the captured Possible surfaces as evidence, not as a slideshow. Keep every frame on the warm cream/ink/coral system from `frame.md`, with one focal statement and one coral voltage moment per frame. Let typography arrive sequentially across the back half of each shot, then hold still long enough to read. Use clean cuts and restrained crossfades; no ambient drift, bouncy motion, decorative noise, narration, music, or SFX. Frames 2 and 5 are deliberate held reads; all other frames use paced reveals and settle before the cut. Never front-load the whole canvas or let unrelated elements float like a screensaver.
