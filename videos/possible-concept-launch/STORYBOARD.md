## Video direction

Make the first beat feel expansive, not anxious: a few real categories of work
move through the frame, then resolve into one clear ask. Possible is the narrowing
move. Keep the visual language warm cream / ink / coral with a single coral voltage
moment per frame. Use captured Possible surfaces as evidence of the concrete path,
not as a deck of screenshots. No narration, music, SFX, ambient drift, bouncy
motion, invented metrics, or browser chrome. Give each statement enough stillness
to read before the next idea arrives.
