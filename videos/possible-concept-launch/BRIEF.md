## Intent

Introduce Possible to builders who can ask Codex for almost anything but need help
choosing a direction and turning an open-ended idea into work that can actually be
run and inspected. The film should feel like a clean product launch: possibility
first, then a simple concrete path, then a low-pressure invitation to visit
possible.sh.
